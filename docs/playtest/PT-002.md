## Bugs found & fixed during this playtest (all in PR #8)

1. **StarterGui respawn duplication** — Roblox re-clones client scripts into PlayerGui on every respawn; mid-run deaths stacked duplicate GUIs (overlapping panels, stale welcome card, garbled HUD). Fixed: idempotency guards (SF_*Loaded attributes) on all StarterGui scripts.
2. **WaveComplete vs GameOver race** — defeat on the wave-9 clear tick could land WaveComplete AFTER GameOver, re-opening the rest panel over the results screen. Fixed: server-side `gameActive` guard before notifying wave completion.
3. **TEST_MODE watcher latch** — the fixture watcher marked itself applied before any player registered, silently skipping the veteran HP grant. Fixed: latch only on a real grant + console confirmation line.
4. **runWaves ignored TEST_START_WAVE** — `for wave = 1, maxWaves` hardcoded; start()'s assignment was dead. Fixed: loop begins at clamped TEST_START_WAVE.
5. **EnemySpawner never set Rarity** — every kill paid as `common` (1💎); boss paid 1 instead of its 50 bounty. Fixed: Rarity attribute set at spawn; boss tier corrected to `boss` (50) in EnemyData.
6. **GameOver panel-close missed clones** — first-match-only close left stale panels. Fixed: iterate all clones (superseded by guards, kept for robustness).

## Balance observations (Phase 6 tuning input, no changes made)

- **Wave 9 is a systematic wall for solo auto-battle**: 4 consecutive defeats (58–60 kills each) with a single rare-power nova spell + 120 HP. Composition wraith×4 (ranged) + spider×4 overwhelms a stationary wizard. Intended design: crafting (2+ spells) or co-op is the answer. Tuning knobs ready: wraith count, wraith range, nova cooldown.
- **Boss math**: Colossus 500 HP, stomp 15/3s + rain 8×4/5s ≈ 11.4 DPS vs wizard 120 HP → ~10.5s survival at full HP; wizard needs ~56s at nova DPS to kill it solo. Boss is a co-op/checkpoint fight by design; the Phase-3 MaxHP unlock + crafted spells close the gap in real play.
- **WaveRestUI shop labels compress** when panel height shrinks (cosmetic, pre-existing).
- **Welcome card dismissal** is wired to wave-1 completion only; TEST_START_WAVE runs skip it (harness artifact; a timeout fallback is a polish candidate).
